| 2026-08-21 | **Admin Users management — create, edit, change password, delete.** Backend: `user.service.js` (new) adds `create`/`update`/`updatePassword`/`remove` alongside the existing `list`, wired through new `POST /users`, `PUT /users/:id`, `PUT /users/:id/password`, `DELETE /users/:id` routes (admin/superadmin only). Role is immutable after creation; only a superadmin may create/edit/password-reset/delete a superadmin account (403 otherwise, and `list` hides superadmin rows from a non-superadmin's unfiltered/all-role query) — deliberately stricter than the CKQ reference this was adapted from. Delete guards: parent blocked by existing children, student blocked by `Registration`/`Subscription`/`TrialClass`, coach blocked by `GroupClassSchedule`; admin/superadmin have no entity guard.
